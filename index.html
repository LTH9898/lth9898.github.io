About

Taehoon Lee 이태훈

I am an M.S. student at KAIST, in the MLV Lab (School of Computing), advised by Prof. Hyunwoo J. Kim.

My interests are generative models, representation learning, and world models. More recently I have been drawn to embodied AI — representations for robots, and post-training.

I plan to use this site to write up what I am studying and share what I find interesting along the way.

If any of this overlaps with your own work, I would be glad to talk — feel free to reach out for a coffee chat by email or on LinkedIn. :)
